ADAGACHE FOUNDATION

Adagache Foundation is a beacon of hope and compassion, a place where love finds expression through service to humanity. Established by Adagache Homes Estate under the visionary leadership of Adagache John Abbah, the foundation is driven by a deep commitment to making a difference in the lives of the less privileged.

Through your kind support, interventions stretch across immediate community care, basic provisions, and educational structural access, establishing long-term lifelines for underrepresented groups in Nigeria.
